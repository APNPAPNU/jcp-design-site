    06 FAQ

    Good questions.

    How fast is turnaround?

    Most projects are delivered within a few days of you approving the mockup. Bigger projects like websites take a little longer, and you’ll get a clear timeline before we start. Have a deadline? Tell me up front.

    How do revisions work?

    Small revisions are unlimited: text edits, color tweaks, swapping a photo, nudging the layout. We keep refining until you’re happy. If you decide to go in a completely new direction, we’ll talk it through first.

    What do you need from me?

    Your business name, what the design is for, and any text or info that should appear. If you have them, send your current logo, photos, colors you like and a few examples of designs you admire. Don’t have much? No problem. I’ll guide you.

    How does payment work?

    Prices are flat and agreed before work begins. Typically there’s a deposit to get started and the balance is due on delivery, payable through common methods. We’ll confirm the details together before anything starts.

    Is the mockup really free?

    Yes. Tell me about your project and I’ll put together a quick concept so you can see the direction. There’s no obligation to continue.

    Do you work with businesses outside Niagara Falls?

    Yes. I’m based in Niagara Falls, NY and work with small businesses anywhere in the US and beyond. Everything happens online (email, text, phone or a quick video call) and files are delivered digitally. For print pieces you get print-ready files you can take to any local or online printer.

    How does business listing setup work?

    I add or update your hours, services or menu, photos and description on Google Business Profile, Yelp and Tripadvisor. You stay the owner of every listing. You can add me as a manager, or we can do it together on a quick call. If a site asks the owner to verify the business (Google often does), you’ll complete that step and I’ll walk you through it.
